What Can You Sell?

We evaluate and collect a comprehensive range of personal and business electronic items:

  • ✔ Laptops & Notebooks: All brands, working or faulty.
  • ✔ Smartphones & Tablets: Android, iOS, functional screens.
  • ✔ Desktop Computers & CPUs: Complete towers or cabinets.
  • ✔ Monitors & Displays: LCD, LED screens in working order.
  • ✔ Printers & Scanners: Inkjet, laser, desktop printers.
  • ✔ Networking Hardware: Wi-Fi routers, modems, switches.
  • ✔ Internal Components: RAM sticks, GPUs, hard drives, SSDs.
  • ✔ Chargers & Accessories: Keyboards, mice, adapters.

🔒 Data Safety Tip

Please remember to back up personal files and execute a factory reset on your device prior to submitting items.
