E-Waste Buy & Sell Policy

Operational guidelines, item condition standards, data privacy duties, and sample pricing terms.

📜 Portfolio Disclaimer: This policy is a static front-end document designed for website layout demonstration.

1. Accepted Electronic Items

E-Solution Hub provides guidelines for trading in or submitting a wide selection of consumer and office electronics, including:

  • Desktop Computers & Server Towers
  • Laptops, Notebooks & Ultrabooks
  • Smartphones, Feature Phones & Tablets
  • Monitors (LCD, LED) & Display Units
  • Desktop Printers, Scanners & All-in-One Units
  • Wi-Fi Routers, Modems & Switches
  • Internal Computer Components (RAM, GPUs, SSDs, HDDs)
  • Chargers, Adapters, Cables & Peripherals

2. Product Condition Assessment

Users submitting devices are required to provide accurate, honest representations regarding:

  • Current functional and boot status
  • Physical wear, screen cracks, or chassis damage
  • Missing accessories, power adapters, or internal components
  • Estimated age, brand, and exact model number

3. Data Privacy & User Responsibilities

Device owners hold sole responsibility for data removal prior to submission. Users must perform:

  • Complete personal data backup to cloud or external drive
  • Sign-out of user accounts (Google, Apple ID, Microsoft, iCloud)
  • Factory reset and data wiping of primary storage
  • Removal of SIM cards, MicroSD cards, and physical media

4. Sample & Portfolio Pricing Terms

Prices displayed across the marketplace are sample / demo figures created for project demonstration purposes. Real-world device valuation relies upon factors such as item condition, component market demand, brand value, working status, and secondary refurbishing costs.

5. Prohibited Materials

We do not accept hazardous non-electronic materials, unsealed liquid chemicals, bio-hazardous waste, or radioactive industrial equipment through ordinary e-waste collection channels.

6. Responsible Recycling Commitment

Items determined to be beyond economic repair or refurbishment are designated for certified material recovery partners to reclaim precious metals (copper, gold, aluminum) while isolating hazardous elements (lead, mercury) from municipal soil and water supplies.

7. Ownership & User Guarantees

Users submitting hardware must be the rightful legal owner of the items or hold explicit authorization from their organization to transfer or recycle the equipment.
